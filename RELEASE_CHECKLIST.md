# Release checklist

Author checklist for maintaining this version. The completed technical checks
and their limits are recorded in VALIDATION.json.

- Source and processed-data allowlists are explicit in the preparation records.
- Original software license: MIT; original processed-output license: CC BY 4.0.
- Preserve third-party notices; final-review dispositions are distinct from author publication approval.
- Check source integrity and candidate/feature-order provenance.
- Public-checkpoint readability: PASS; full-system inference remains untested.
- Prediction, archived-analysis and figure-data checks: PASS within VALIDATION.json scope.
- Fresh NumPy environment installation: PASS. Plot recipe resolution: PASS.
  Full training/fragment/MCES environment installations remain untested.
- Review every prepared asset and its SHA256; publish only approved files.
- Assign the actual public release tag/commit and download URLs after review.
- Update CITATION.cff and manuscript availability statements with verified locations.

No DOI or Zenodo deposit is required by the author's chosen hosting plan.
